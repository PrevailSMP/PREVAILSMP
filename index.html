About the server

Built to outlast a season

PrevailSMP started as a small server for friends and has grown into a whitelisted community focused on long builds, a player-driven economy, and keeping the world intact. Here's what that looks like day to day.

01

Player-run economy

Earn, trade, and shop through a full in-game economy. Check the leaderboard at spawn to see who's on top this week.

02

Protected builds

Claim your land and keep it safe from griefing. What you build is yours — permanently, not just until the next wipe.

03

Active moderation

A small, hands-on admin team keeps things fair and answers reports quickly, in-game and on Discord.

04

Community events

Seasonal builds, PvP arenas, and community projects run regularly — check Discord for what's coming up next.

Server rules

Read before you join

These apply to everyone, every rank, every time. Breaking them gets you warned, then punished — ignorance isn't an excuse once you're whitelisted.

General conduct

  1. 01

    No griefing, stealing, or destroying builds outside claimed land rules. This includes lava, TNT, and water griefing in unclaimed areas near spawn.

  2. 02

    Be respectful in chat and voice. No harassment, hate speech, or targeted insults.

  3. 03

    No cheating: no X-ray, hacked clients, or exploiting bugs for personal gain. Found a bug? Report it to staff instead — you won't be punished for reporting.

  4. 04

    Advertising other servers or services is not allowed in chat or Discord.

Building & land

  1. 05

    Claim your base as soon as possible — unclaimed builds aren't protected.

  2. 06

    Keep builds a reasonable distance from spawn and other players' claims unless invited.

  3. 07

    No large-scale terrain destruction without staff approval for server-visible projects.

PvP & economy

  1. 08

    PvP is allowed everywhere except marked safe zones and spawn.

  2. 09

    Scamming other players in trades is bannable — screenshot agreements for high-value trades.

  3. 10

    Duping items or currency through any method results in an immediate ban.

Ready to apply?

Whitelist applications are handled through Discord — join the server, read #how-to-join, and submit your application there.
